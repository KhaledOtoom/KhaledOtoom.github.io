FAQ

Good questions, clear answers.

Every implementation begins by checking the fit with your current process and tools.

What can the system integrate with?

Potential connections include your website forms or chat, messaging channels, CRM and calendar. The exact setup depends on the tools you use, their available connection options and the workflow we agree together.

Do we need to change our CRM?

Usually the goal is to work with your existing CRM where a suitable integration is available. We review your current setup before recommending any changes.

Can it handle Arabic and English?

The planned conversation experience supports Arabic and English. We confirm channel behavior, tone, dialect preferences and testing requirements during discovery.

How long does setup take?

Timing depends on the number of lead sources, integrations, approval steps and edge cases. After reviewing your workflow, we can define a practical scope and timeline.

Can a person take over at any time?

Yes. Human handoff and takeover are part of the design. We agree when the system should pause, who receives the conversation and what context they need.

How do you approach data and privacy?

We map what information is needed, where it should go and who needs access as part of scoping. Data handling depends on the channels and services selected; no security certification or legal compliance claim is implied here.

Can the questions and booking process be customized?

Yes. Qualification fields, approved FAQs, working hours, appointment types, handoff rules and follow-up timing can be planned around your business.
