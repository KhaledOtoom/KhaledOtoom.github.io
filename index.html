FEATURED OFFER

AI Lead Qualification & Routing System

The Problem

Your marketing drives inbound leads, but they arrive with varying intent and budgets. Your sales team wastes hours manually reviewing and filtering them, causing the hottest prospects to cool down before they get a response.

The Solution

An intelligent routing system that catches leads the second they arrive, evaluates them exactly like your best salesperson would, and instantly hands the most valuable opportunities to your team.

How It Works

  • 1. Captures incoming leads from your website or ad platforms.
  • 2. AI analyzes the prospect's budget, timeline, and request.
  • 3. Assigns a 0-100 Lead Score based on your ideal client profile.
  • 4. Sorts leads into "Qualified" (ready to close) or "Nurture" (not quite ready).

What You Get

Your sales team gets an instant mobile alert the moment a qualified prospect reaches out, complete with their score and AI summary. Less time sifting, more time selling.
