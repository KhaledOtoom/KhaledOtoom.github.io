AI Lead Qualification & Routing System

Automatically capture, qualify, score, and route inbound leads before your sales team wastes time on low-intent prospects.

View Live Demo Let's Talk

The Problem

  • ✗ Leads arrive with completely different levels of intent and budget.
  • ✗ Sales teams waste valuable hours manually reviewing and filtering bad data.
  • ✗ High-intent prospects cool down while waiting for a response in a crowded inbox.
  • ✗ Businesses need automatic qualification and routing to scale operations efficiently.

The Solution

  • ✓ Captures incoming leads directly from websites, forms, or ad platforms.
  • ✓ Normalizes, validates, and strictly filters out duplicate records instantly.
  • ✓ Uses AI to analyze intent, budget, timeline, and service fit based on the request.
  • ✓ Assigns a 0–100 lead score and classifies them accurately (Qualified/Nurture).
  • ✓ Logs everything seamlessly into your centralized CRM or Google Sheets.
  • ✓ Sends an instant Telegram alert to sales the moment a high-value lead arrives.
